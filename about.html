About Time Pieces

Our Story

Time Pieces is a family-owned business, located in the heart of Roscommon Town in Ireland. Established in 1992 by Vincent and Mary Brennan, with their daughter Lorna, Time Pieces has happily been bringing our customers the very best and most beautiful in jewellery, furnishings and unusual objects from Ireland and around the world for over 34 years.

Our Values

We take pride in our shop and in our customer satisfaction, which is most important to us. We value our customers and always strive to bring you the best and most unusual with every passing season. Our approach is simple: find beautiful things, tell their stories, and help you find something that speaks to you.

Award-Winning Excellence

Time Pieces is an award-winning shop, voted the 'Best Christmas Window in Ireland' by our customers as part of a nationwide competition for window shopping. This recognition reflects our commitment to creating beautiful, inspiring displays and helping our community celebrate special moments.

Always Evolving

Just as fashions and trends change, so too does our shopping experience. Our new website aims to bring you an experience of Time Pieces online, allowing you to browse our collection from anywhere. We will be constantly adding to our website and including new customer experiences as technologies and trends change.

Visit Us

Come visit us at our shop on Main Street in Roscommon, or explore our collection online. Whether you're looking for a special gift, an heirloom piece, or simply something beautiful for yourself, Time Pieces is your destination for unique treasures.

Thank you for visiting us online and we look forward to serving you both online and in our bricks and mortar shop.
