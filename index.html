Meet the Family

Time Pieces was established in 1992 by Vincent and Mary Brennan, with their daughter Lorna. For over 34 years, we've been bringing our customers the very best and most beautiful in jewellery, furnishings and unusual objects.

We take pride in customer satisfaction and value each visitor. Every season, we bring new treasures to our shop and now to your screen.

Our Award

We were voted the Best Christmas Window in Ireland by our customers as part of a nationwide competition for window shopping.
